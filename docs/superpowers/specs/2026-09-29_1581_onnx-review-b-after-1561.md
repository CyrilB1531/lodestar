# The Onnx findings of the Review B after #1561

**Issues:** [#1581](https://github.com/CyrilB1531/lodestar/issues/1581),
[#1582](https://github.com/CyrilB1531/lodestar/issues/1582).
**Status:** written with the work, 2026-09-29.
**Date:** 2026-09-29.

## The problem

The Review B of `Lodestar.Onnx` after #1561, on `main` at `bf4f2458`, found two defects:

- `EmbedBatch` over texts reached #1555's chunk refusal through the encoder overload, which named
  `encoder`, a parameter the texts overload does not have.
- The pages promised a refusal "before allocating it" without saying the bound is one array's
  length: below it the two chunk buffers are allocated, which can exhaust memory, and on .NET
  Framework one object stops at 2 GB.

## Decisions

- **Each overload names its own argument.** Both text overloads share one private body that
  takes the name to refuse under; the texts overload passes `options`, the source of the width,
  as it already does for #1423's `MaxLength` refusal.
- **The bound is stated, not lowered.** A limit below `Array.MaxLength` would be a guess at the
  machine's memory; the pages say what a chunk below the bound costs instead.
