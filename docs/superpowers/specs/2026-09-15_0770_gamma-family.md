## Problem

A positive, continuous, right-skewed response — a cost, a duration, a concentration — has a spread that
grows with its mean. OLS on it misstates every standard error, and OLS on its logarithm estimates the
mean of the log rather than the log of the mean. The Gamma GLM is the standard answer, and it is the first
family here whose dispersion is **estimated**: `GlmSummary.Dispersion` has read "fixed at 1 … estimated
when a Gamma family lands" since #616.

## Scope

- `sm.GLM(y, X, family=sm.families.Gamma(link=...)).fit()` with the **inverse** link (the default) and
  the **log** link, at `statsmodels` 0.15.0 parity over every number `GlmSummary` carries.
- **Out:** the identity link, `scale="dev"` or a fixed scale, inverse Gaussian and Tweedie (0115),
  `var_weights`, offsets (#777).

## What the reference does, measured

Read from `statsmodels/genmod` 0.15.0 and run from `/var/tmp`:

1. **Link.** The default is `InversePower` (`η = 1/μ`), the canonical link, constructed with a
   `DomainWarning` because nothing keeps `1/η` positive. `Log` is the one link statsmodels calls safe.
   Neither link clips `μ`.
2. **Variance** `μ²`; **deviance** `2 Σ [ −log(y/μ) + (y − μ)/μ ]`, with `y/μ` clipped at machine epsilon.
3. **Scale.** Pearson's `Σ (y − μ)² / μ² / df_resid`, read at the converged mean. The covariance is the
   last IRLS solve's, multiplied by it; the Wald table still reads the normal (`use_t` is false). Measured:
   `0.052633144617514054` against a hand Pearson sum of `0.052633144617514116`.
4. **Log-likelihood** at that scale: `Σ [ (1/φ) log(y/(μφ)) − y/(μφ) − lnΓ(1/φ) − log y ]`. The **AIC**
   is `2k − 2ℓ` with `k` the coefficients alone — the estimated scale is not counted (`30.7715` both ways).
5. **Start** `(y + ȳ)/2`; **null deviance** at `μ = ȳ` (a constant-only fit reaches `4.96` against a mean
   of `4.959999999999999`). **Stop** on the absolute change of the deviance *divided by the scale*, and the
   scale it divides by is the Pearson estimate at the previous iteration's mean — `_fit_irls` passes
   `self.scale` to `family.deviance` in `_update_history` before re-estimating it. Found while replaying:
   stopping on the unscaled deviance ended the Gamma fits one iteration off, 1e-7 to 2e-6 relative on the
   coefficients. For the fixed-scale families the scale is exactly 1 and nothing moves.
6. **A zero response** fits: the deviance clips it, and the log-likelihood is `+inf`.

## Placement — a family member, and a link option

- **`GlmFamily.Gamma`**, a new member, as `NegativeBinomial` joined in #769.
- **`GlmOptions.Link`, a new `GlmLink` enum: `Default`, `Log`, `Inverse`.** The link is a second axis
  that the family enum cannot carry without multiplying members (`GammaLog`, `GammaInverse`, and #777's
  offsets would multiply them again). `Default` is each family's statsmodels default, so every existing
  call is unchanged. A link a family does not take here — `Inverse` for `Poisson`, `Log` for `Binomial`
  — is refused on `options`, as #769 refuses an `alpha` given to the wrong family. `Log` is accepted for
  `Poisson` and `NegativeBinomial`, whose default it already is.
- **Rejected:** a `link` parameter on `Fit` (a fifth positional argument every call would pass through),
  and link members on `GlmFamily`.

## Behaviour

- **Parity** on points 1 to 5, the lagged-scale criterion included.
- **`GlmSummary.Dispersion`** is the Pearson scale for `Gamma` and stays 1 for the other three; its
  documentation stops promising a future.
- **Refused**, each recorded in `docs/equivalence.md`:
  - a response that is not **finite and above zero** (`ArgumentException` on `response`), where the
    reference fits a zero to an infinite log-likelihood (point 6);
  - a fitted mean that is **not above zero** under the inverse link, at the iteration it happens
    (`ArgumentException` on `design`, naming the link and suggesting `Log`), where the reference
    carries on with `|μ|` in its variance and a clipped deviance;
  - a link the family does not take here.
- **The start** takes Poisson's arm, and `Clamp` leaves a Gamma mean alone, as the reference does.

## Evidence

- `stats_glm.json` gains a `gamma` block: inverse and log links; with and without an intercept; two
  regressors; a 99% level; a response spanning two orders of magnitude. Values moderate enough that the
  scale-weighted log-likelihood holds the reproducibility gate's absolute `1e-9`. The other blocks
  regenerate unchanged.
- Edge tests for each refusal, `Default` equal to `Inverse` exactly for `Gamma` and to `Log` for
  `Poisson`, and `Dispersion` equal to a hand Pearson sum.
