## The problem

The Review B of `Lodestar.Onnx` after #1532, on `main` at `54e85920`, found four defects:

- `MaxSequenceLength` answered from its cached value after `Dispose`, though the page says every
  member throws `ObjectDisposedException`.
- No test pinned the disposed object's full name, which #1527 fixed on netstandard2.0.
- No test pinned the `OnnxRuntimeException` #1524 documented for a missing or unreadable model.
- A static export's chunk buffer was sized `chunk * width` without the `TableLength` bound, so a
  graph declaring `[65536, 32768]` failed with `OverflowException` instead of a named refusal.

## Decisions

- **`MaxSequenceLength` throws after `Dispose`.** Answering is harmless, but the page states one
  rule for every member; the rule is kept and the property made to follow it.
- **The chunk is refused under the caller's parameter.** `TableLength.Of` names the same parameter
  the sequence-length refusal already names, so `Embed` reports `inputIds` and the batch overloads
  their own argument.
- **A declared, never-run fixture.** `tiny_embedder_huge_static.onnx` is the static export with its
  axes fixed at `[65536, 32768]`: 1,244 bytes, since a declared shape costs nothing until a run.
  It is written by `tools/build_tiny_models.py`, whose `[2, 16]` export regenerates byte for byte.
