# 0830 — The exact Kolmogorov-Smirnov table in two rows

**Status:** accepted, 2026-09-16. Written after the measurement it records.

Issue: [#830](https://github.com/CyrilB1531/lodestar/issues/830), found by a performance review of `main`.

## Change

The exact Kolmogorov-Smirnov table walk swaps two rows instead of allocating one per step.

## Measured

| sizes (`ExactMethod.Exact`) | `main` | fix |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 99 × 101 | 38.3 µs, 84.5 KB | 30.5 µs, 4.1 KB |
| 999 × 1,001 | 3.03 ms, 7.9 MB | **2.17 ms, 39 KB** |

Same expressions in the same order: p-values bit-identical against `main` for six size pairs under all three alternatives. `KsExactTableBenchmarks`, pinned to four cores.

## Rejected

- **A change that moves results.** Each fix keeps the corpus replaying at its tolerance, and was compared against `main` on inputs the corpus does not hold.
