## The problem

The Review B of `Lodestar.Gpu` after #1563, on `main` at `f0706f28`, found three defects:

- `TiledCosineTopK.Search` sized its hit buffers `queryCount × take` in `long` and read them back
  into one host array after both launches, the sibling #1558 fixed for `Signatures` and `Multiply`.
- The three-argument `Signatures` overload's XML left out the refusal it inherits.
- The span overload of `Multiply` copied and uploaded the whole block before the resident overload
  refused the product, and named `block` although `width` makes the product large.

## Decisions

- **Refused before the first copy.** `Search` refuses `queryCount × take` past one array under `k`,
  after the query checks and before the queries are staged; the span `Multiply` refuses
  `rows × width` under `width` before the upload. The resident `Multiply` keeps its own refusal
  under `block`, since a caller can reach it with a block already resident.
- **Tested on the CPU accelerator.** One-dimensional rows and queries reach 2.29e9 hits from a few
  hundred kilobytes; an empty CSR matrix reaches the product bound the same way.
