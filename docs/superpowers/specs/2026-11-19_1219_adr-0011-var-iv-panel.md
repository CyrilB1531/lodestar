# Decision 0011: vector autoregression, instrumental variables and panel regression are written here

**Issue:** [#1219](https://github.com/CyrilB1531/lodestar/issues/1219).
**Status:** written with the work, 2026-11-20; split out of the branch that also carries #1784 to #1790.
**Date:** 2026-11-19.

## The problem

[Decision 0004](../../decisions/0004-what-is-written-here-and-what-is-delegated.md) records the vector
autoregression, instrumental variables and the panel estimators as **not written**. All three ship:
`VectorAutoregression` since #786, `InstrumentalVariables` since #1155 and `PanelRegression` since #1156.
`VectorAutoregression` and `InstrumentalVariables` cite 0004 for why they are written, while the rows they cite say
they are not. The review of `main` before 1.0.0 found the record contradicting the code that cites it.

## Decisions

- **A new record amends 0004's three rows to *written here*.** Each keeps the measurement 0004 gave it.
  [Decision 0011](../../decisions/0011-vector-autoregression-instrumental-variables-and-panel-regression-are-written-here.md)
  carries `amends: ["0004"]`. For those three rows only, it amends the verdicts, 0004's statement that its
  verdicts were taken by 2026-09-16, the consequence that a caller reopens them, and its caller question; it makes
  no claim about any other row. 0134 and 0135 left each lot to an issue "if Cyril wants" it, and Cyril opened
  #786, #1155 and #1156; none names a caller. 0004 itself is not edited, because a record is
  never edited (`tools/check_adr_immutable.py`).
- **The index and the records' README follow.** `index.yaml` is regenerated by `tools/regen_adr_index.py`, so 0004
  gains the `amended_by` edge back. `docs/decisions/README.md` lists the record, and `tools/tests/test_adr_index.py`
  counts eleven records.
- **A citation of 0004 for one of the three verdicts also cites 0011.** These sites cite both:
  - the XML remarks of `VectorAutoregression` and `InstrumentalVariables`, which cited 0004 already, and a new
    sentence in `PanelRegression`'s remarks, which cited no decision before;
  - the reference pages `var.md`, `iv.md` and `panel.md`;
  - the root `README.md`'s sentence on what decides each case, which now names 0008 and 0011 as 0004's amendments;
  - the statsmodels migration page: a sentence of its own after the summary, which takes the VAR, IV and panel
    verdicts out of the list 0004's readings settled, the summary keeping one of a duplicated pair of 0004 links; and
    its VAR, IV and panel rows. The IV row also gives 0004's `6.6e-6`
    for iterated GMM.

  A sentence that cites 0004 for an absence keeps that citation, with the verdict's 0011 citation beside it. The
  iterated-GMM row of `docs/equivalence.md` keeps 0004 alone: 0011 repeats that verdict without amending it, and
  it rests on a measurement.
- **No behaviour changes.** This branch changes documentation and documentation comments only. The exact-fit, panel,
  IV and GMM findings #1784 to #1790 stay parked on `fix/1219-regression-review-findings`. That branch is to drop its
  copy of 0011 and its #1219 claims when it is reworked.
