# The Review B after #1774: exact fits, the time-series scale and two scans

**Issues:** [#1779](https://github.com/CyrilB1531/lodestar/issues/1779).
**Status:** written with the work, 2026-11-13.
**Date:** 2026-11-13.

## The problem

The Review B after [#1774](https://github.com/CyrilB1531/lodestar/pull/1774) found five things on `main` at 7e0ad932.
On `y = 3 + 2·x₁ − x₂`, integers and quarters, every residual is rounding, and the robust Wald tests read it: HC0's F
1.4e36 with p = 0, the IV kernel test 5.3e32, the panel's robust test 2.8e33, and GMM inverted a weight of rounding,
where `OlsSummary`, the IV and GMM pages and [#1225](2026-11-08_1225_stats-regression-review-findings.md)'s second
rule promise NaN or a refusal. With two singleton columns, the rows they pick out have a leverage of one within
rounding, and HC2 divided a squared rounding by a rounding, F = 1.8e14 on three degrees of freedom where statsmodels
reads 80.29 on two; examined, HC2 and HC3 are kept as statsmodels computes them (the Decision on HC2 and HC3 below).
KPSS, the autocorrelations and Ljung-Box read NaN at a
scale of 2^600 or 2^-1000, and 0 at 2^-535, where ADF reads the same statistic at every scale. And two scans cost: the
finiteness scan took 20 to 29% of `OLS.Estimate` at 100 × 3, and ADF copied its series on nearly every call, 8% and
1.6 KB at 200 points.

## Decisions

- **A fit is exact when every residual, its part in the regressors' span removed, lies within its row's rounding.**
  On an exact fit `r = −X·δb + ρ`: `δb`, the solve's rounding of the coefficients, lies in the span, and `ρ`, the
  rounding of computing `y − Xb`, within `(k + 1)ε` of each row's terms, `mⱼ = |rⱼ| + Σ|xⱼₗbₗ|`, or of the floor a
  panel's transform leaves the row: the largest row as given of its entity, of its period under time effects, the
  grand means' size where the constant's go back in, either row a difference subtracts. Removing the span, `Hr`
  with `H = QQᵀ`, leaves `ρ − Hρ`, so `ExactFit.Holds` requires `|(r − Hr)ᵢ| ≤ (k + 1)ε·(mᵢ + Σⱼ|Hᵢⱼ|mⱼ)`, a bound
  on `|ρᵢ − (Hρ)ᵢ|` whatever `ρ`'s signs. It reads it in steps, each answering only what its bound decides, with
  `M = max|r| + max|x|·Σ|bⱼ|`, the largest residual plus the largest regressor times the coefficients' sizes, an upper
  bound on every `mⱼ`, a transform's floors included. A generalized fit is read on its rows as given, `y − X·b`:
  whitening by `L⁻¹` mixes the rows, so a whitened residual is the rounding of its neighbours' terms as well as its
  own, and read against its own whitened row it missed the exact AR(1) fit at `ρ = 0.9`, 200 rows, HC1's F 3.5e32
  (Review A pass 15); the panel's transforms keep their floors, which already bound the rows a transform mixes. `Q`
  is the rows' own: Householder reflections of the rows, each column scaled to unit size, built past the raw reject
  only. The computed `Q̂` lies within `√k·γ` of an orthonormal `Q̃` spanning rows `X + ΔX`, `‖ΔX‖₂ ≤ γ‖R‖_F`, with
  `γ = 4nkε` Higham's `γ̃ₙₖ` (*Accuracy and Stability*, §19.3, Theorem 19.4) taken with its unstated constant `c` as
  8, and by Wedin's theorem `Q̃Q̃ᵀ` lies within
  `η/(1 − η)` of `H`, `η = ‖ΔX‖₂‖R⁻¹‖₂ ≤ γκ`, `κ = ‖R‖_F‖R⁻¹‖_F` of the scaled columns' triangle; so `(I − H)v`,
  `H·m`, `hᵢᵢ` and `qᵢ·qⱼ` err by at most `γ(κ + 2√k)/(1 − γκ)` times `‖v‖₂` or `‖m‖₂`, and from `γκ = ½` the test
  refuses, main's answer (Review A passes 18, 19); where `v − X(XᵀX)⁻¹Xᵀv` carried `κ²`: on `x₂ = x₁ + d·u`, `y = 1 + x₁ + 2x₂`, five seeds at 50 and 200 rows, pass 15 kept main's
  answer from `d = 1e-9`, HC1's F −2.5e15 at `1e-11`, and its inversion of `XᵀX` threw on every GLS fit (Review A
  pass 16). The span's part comes off first, `r′ = r − X·δ` with `δ = R⁻¹Qᵀr`, compensated as Ogita, Rump and
  Oishi's Dot2, so it errs by `2ε|r′ᵢ| + (2(k + 1)ε)²` of its terms: a normal-equations fit's residual holds `κ·ε` of
  its coefficients in the span, and the reflections' rounding read off the whole of it let robust 2SLS call noise
  four times the rule's bound exact (Review A pass 17). A row is exact only where proven within the rule, every
  rounding of the test counted against it, so a fit whose `γ·κ·√n`, roughly, nears one is not called exact, the
  margin being that rounding times `‖m‖₂` and `‖r′‖₂`, about `√n` of a row's size; a column with
  nothing left past the others ends the test false, main's answer:
  - a raw reject, a lane scan before anything is built: the raw residual still holds `X·δb`. An OLS, WLS or GLS
    residual, from a QR, backward stable, or normal equations held to a scaled condition number of 200, rejects past
    256 times the screen's bound, `(k + 1)ε·(1 + √n)·M`: every exact fit measured reached at most 0.74 of that
    bound, and relative noise of `1e-10` at 2,000 × 8 reaches 431, `1e-9` 4,310 and a response stored in single precision 4.1e5, where under `2²⁰`
    they paid the reflections, 1.38 times main (Review A pass 17). An IV, GMM or panel residual rejects past `2²⁰`
    times it plus `4κ·(k + 1)ε·M`, `κ = ‖A‖₁‖A⁻¹‖₁` of the matrix its normal equations inverted, whose forward error
    `κ·ε` carries into `δb`: 2SLS at `d = 1e-4` read its model test at 4.8e7 and the entity-effects panel at 5.1e14
    (Review A pass 16), and the exact 2SLS fits reached `3.1κ·ε·M`. The one step that can reject an exact fit, which
    then keeps main's answer;
  - a screen, `O(n·k)` after `(I − H)r`: `Σⱼ|Hᵢⱼ|mⱼ ≤ √hᵢᵢ·‖m‖₂ ≤ √n·M`, so a row past `(k + 1)ε·(mᵢ + √n·M)` rejects,
    the first row of almost any noisy fit (a transformed fit takes `M` for `mᵢ`);
  - a lower bound, `O(n·k)`: `|Σⱼ Hᵢⱼmⱼ| ≤ Σⱼ|Hᵢⱼ|mⱼ`, and `H·m = Q·Qᵀm`, so a row within
    `(k + 1)ε·(mᵢ + |(H·m)ᵢ|)` passes, almost every row of an exact fit;
  - for the rows left, a tighter lower and an upper bound, `O(n·k²)` once: the rows that each carry more than a
    sixteenth of `T = Σⱼ√hⱼⱼ·mⱼ`, sixteen at most, read pair by pair, with `|Σⱼ Hᵢⱼmⱼ|` over the others, bound the sum
    from below, and `H` being a projection, `|Hᵢⱼ| ≤ √(hᵢᵢhⱼⱼ)` and `Σⱼ Hᵢⱼ² = hᵢᵢ` bound the others from above by
    `√hᵢᵢ` times the smaller of their `Σ√hⱼⱼ·mⱼ` and their `‖m‖₂`; a row within the lower passes, past the upper fails;
  - only a row between those reads all its pairs, `O(n·k)`, and the first that fails ends the test.

## Settled

- **Noise within the rounding bound reads as exact.** A residual within `(k + 1)ε` of its row's terms and what the
  span can carry in is within the first-order bound of the rounding of computing it, and is read as zero. The bound is
  an upper one, so noise below it is not separated from rounding; none of the 300 random noisy fits came within it.
- **A false negative keeps main's answer.** A fit whose rounding the bound does not cover, residuals past their bound
  though zero in exact arithmetic, keeps the noise it read before; the bound is not widened to reach
  it, which would call fits with real noise exact.
- **One helper per quantity.** `ExactFit.Magnitude`, `|rᵢ| + Σⱼ|xᵢⱼbⱼ|`, serves the exact-fit test and the residual
  sizes the reduced-rank bound reads, which keep #1225's per row: its tolerance, `1e-6` of the form, is the one #1225
  measured; HC2's and HC3's divisors are built once and passed to the filling and to its score rows;
  `SeriesChecks.UnitScale` returns the largest magnitude it scanned, which the straight-line refusal reads rather than
  scanning again (Review A pass 1).
- **`ModelTest` is unchanged.** The panel's classical F is a ratio of sums of squares, the reference's too, and an
  exact fit's limit there is the infinity it already approaches; the OLS non-robust F moved, as its Decision above says.
