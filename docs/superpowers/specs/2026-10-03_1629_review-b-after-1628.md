## The problem

The Review B after #1628, on `main` at `84d272cc`, found no defect in what the persistence rework
computes, and three things around it:

- A load from a stream of undeclared length allocated more than the released 0.7.0 from about 1 KB
  to 81,920 bytes, and again just past a mebibyte (#1629). #1624 started the growing segments at
  256 bytes and doubled them, so they summed to about twice the artifact, and joining them copied it
  once more. 0.7.0's `MemoryStream` sized its first buffer to the first read, `max(read, 256)`, and
  handed it over uncopied. Measured as the bytes an undeclared load allocates over a seekable one:
  115,032 at 24,262 bytes and 213,480 at 78,262, against 0.7.0's 81,984 for both. #1624's premise —
  that the stream before started at 256 bytes — held only up to 256 bytes, and its test and
  benchmark measured a 284-byte and a 7,301-byte artifact, under and over the band.
- `CountVectorizer` and `TfidfVectorizer` documented their `Save(string)` refusal of an unfitted
  vectorizer as coming once the file is open, where it comes before the path (#1630).
- No test held #1625's message to the writer's exception type, or its inner exception (#1631).

## Decisions

- **The growing read's first segment grows as `MemoryStream`'s buffer did**: to the larger of what
  the read brings, twice its length and 256 bytes, copied over, up to one array; past it, mebibyte
  segments. An artifact that fits one array is then one array, handed over uncopied, and one read
  of 81,920 bytes or less allocates the artifact once, as 0.7.0 did. A chain of several segments
  now always holds more than one array, so `BuildJoined`, which copied segments that fit one into
  one, could no longer copy anything and is removed. Capping that
  first segment at a mebibyte was tried and dropped: Review A computed, and the test measured,
  3,408,208 bytes over a seekable load at 1,080,263 bytes — the first segment grown to a mebibyte,
  a second linked, both joined — against 2,179,848 on `main` and 1,541,336 on 0.7.0. The read
  buffer, and the byte count a `MaxTotalBytes` refusal names, stay as they were.
- **The test and the benchmark measure the band**: count vectorizers of about 24 KB, 78 KB and
  1.08 MB, the test holding an undeclared load over a seekable one to the read buffer and 16 KB at
  the first two and to 1.6 MB at the third. On `main` it fails at all three, by the figures above.
- **The documentation places each refusal**: the unfitted one before the path is opened, the
  strings once the file is open.
- **No changelog entry**: the allocation and the wording are #1618's and #1628's, unreleased.
